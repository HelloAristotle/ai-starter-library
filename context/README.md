# Your context map

Context is the useful background your AI needs so you do not have to explain everything again. This folder holds your copy of that background.

Start with a few lines. Leave anything you do not need blank. Say **“set up”** in a chat with this workspace open to have the AI help, or edit the files yourself.

## What to read

| File | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| [personal.md](personal.md) | Who you are, how you like to work, your priorities, recurring needs, and useful sources. |
| [company.md](company.md) | What your company does, who it helps, your role, current work, voice, boundaries, and useful sources. |

For a personal task, start with personal.md. For company work, start with company.md and use personal working preferences only when relevant. Read both for a task that needs both. Do not include unrelated personal details in company work.

## Keep the right information here

- **Reusable background goes here.** Roles, preferences, company facts, important constraints, and pointers to current information.
- **Live records stay in their usual tools.** Appointments, messages, customer records, and task lists belong in their existing home. Record where to find them instead of copying them all here.
- **Blank means unknown.** Bracketed text describes what could go there; it is not an answer. Your AI should ask or leave it blank.
- **One fact, one home.** Update a fact where it lives. Avoid keeping competing copies in both files.
- **Changes need context.** Use a date for current priorities and changing facts. Identify the source of imported information and label anything still to confirm.

## Grow only when you need to

If a topic becomes large, ask the AI to give it its own clearly named file, such as writing-preferences.md or current-project.md, and add one row to this map explaining when to read it. Keep a short pointer in the original file. There is no need to create those files in advance.

Say **“update my context”** when a fact changes. Ask the AI to show the edited file. Keep filled-in files private and share only what is appropriate for the tool and audience. This folder is not a password store or an automatic connection to your accounts.
