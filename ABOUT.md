# About this library

Hello Aristotle helps people and teams learn AI through their real work. This library brings together our context templates, beginner setup flow, and five reusable methods for a small useful first result.

## Using the materials

The prompts and blank templates are free to use for your own tasks and learning. Keep your filled-in copies in an appropriate private location. The Hello Aristotle name and logo identify the publisher; they do not imply endorsement of adaptations or other services. For republishing or other reuse questions, visit [Hello Aristotle](https://helloaristotle.com).

No MIT, Apache, Creative Commons, or other broad open-source license has been selected for this collection. Public availability and free use of these prompts for your work are distinct from permission to repackage the whole library or branding.

## What is included

These are original Hello Aristotle teaching materials adapted from our existing Context Starter, Client Skills Starter, and AI Starter Library. The named practice scenario is fictional. The public files contain generic instructions and blank templates; they do not include customer records, private teaching-call transcripts, or a learner's context.

## What to expect

The methods guide an AI; they do not guarantee accuracy, tool access, memory, hiring outcomes, or business results. Check important facts and use the finish checks in each guide. You can use the methods by pasting or explicitly reading the files. They are not a preinstalled native skill package.
